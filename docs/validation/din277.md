DIN 277 is licensed. Its text and its table of uses are not in the repository: the office assigns UEA's room uses to the standard's usage groups in the table `din277-uses` from its own copy (`docs/decisions/0008-office-supplied-norm-data.md`). Without the table, BGF, NRF, KGF and BRI are still computed; only the split of the NRF into NUF, TF and VF is missing, and the output says so.

## What it computes

Per storey (Grundrissebene) and for the whole project, or for one storey (`uea calc din277 OG`):

| Value | How |
|---|---|
| BGF | The storey's outline at its outer faces with the finishes: the wall polygons and the storey's slab outlines, filled, grown by the thinnest exterior finish of its exterior walls (mitred, so the finish runs round the corners and over the ends of walls); an exterior wall with a thicker finish adds its own band. Voids in the storey's own slab (Lufträume, a stair hole) are deducted. |
| NRF | Each room's finished outline (between the finished surfaces, `docs/decisions/0015-rooms-and-finishes.md`), minus the voids in it. Installation shafts are not part of a room (decision 0045). |
| NUF 1 to 7, TF, VF | Each room's NRF goes to the group the office's table gives its use. A stair's footprint inside a room, on the storey it starts from, is VF. |
| KGF | BGF − NRF: wall cores and finishes, door reveals, niches, shafts, and floor that has no room in the model (reported separately). |
| BRI | Each storey's BGF outline (voids included) times the height from its finished floor to the finished floor of the storey above. The lowest storey adds the height from the underside of all layers of its floor slab to its finished floor (foundations excluded). The top storey goes from its finished floor up to the top of the roof covering: the roof's planes plus the thickness of the layers above the rafters, integrated exactly over the outline (each piece under one plane: area × height at its centroid). The roof's footprint grows like the outline, so the finish is under the roof too. |

Every result records the calculator, version, model state, scope, settings and the edition of the table used (`out/din277.json`); the report for humans is `out/din277.md`.

## Not implemented

- Areas outside the Regelfall der Raumumschließung (Sonderfall: balconies, loggias, terraces, open covered areas). The model has no such elements yet.
- Courtyards inside a storey's outline are filled, as if enclosed.
- A roof over a storey below the top one (a hall beside a storeyed wing): that storey's slice ends at the finished floor of the storey above, as for any storey but the top one.
- Separate figures by clear height.
- A storey with no roof and no storey above has no BRI; the result says so and is marked incomplete.

## Assumptions to check when signing

- The outline of the attic of Haus Müller: its eaves sides have no wall, so the outline runs along the slab's edge (the outer face of the cores below) grown by the render of the storey's gable walls. That is the outer face of the building below.
- A stair counts as VF on the storey it starts from; the hole above it in the next storey's slab is no floor of that storey (neither BGF nor NRF), but its space is in the BRI.
- Door reveals and niches belong to the KGF, since rooms end at the wall face.
- Installation shafts belong to the KGF.
- The underside of the lowest floor construction is the underside of all layers of the storey's slab, insulation under it included.
