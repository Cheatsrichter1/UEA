# 0051: Reading the bids, comparing them and the Kostenanschlag

Status: Accepted (built)
Date: 2026-10-21

## Context

The LV goes out to bidders as GAEB DA XML, phase 83 (`0048-leistungsverzeichnis.md`). The bids come back as phase 84 (Angebotsabgabe): the same LV with a unit price (EP) and a total price (GP) for each position, and the bid's total and discount. The office then checks the arithmetic, compares the bids position by position (Preisspiegel), recommends one, and after the award the costs per DIN 276 rest on the prices of the bids (Kostenanschlag). Version 2 deferred this part (`0049-cost-estimate-and-hoai-fee.md`).

The arithmetic check has a rule: where the GP of a position is not its quantity times its EP, the EP counts (VOB/A 2019, § 16c Abs. 2 Nr. 1). The rest of the Wertung (suitability, unusually low prices, criteria beyond the price) is the client's decision, made by people.

## Decision

- **A bid lives in the model,** in a new discipline file `bid.uea` downstream of the LV: one `bid` line per bidder and one `price` line per position it prices. Batches, history, revert and `uea get` work on it unchanged; the file is read by code, not by agents.

```
bid bd1 "Muster Bau GmbH" date=2026-11-02 total=23820.96 disc=2 file=muster.x84
price pr1 bd1 p1 ep=110 qty=90.51
price pr6 bd2 p1 ep=100 qty=90.51 gp=9100
```

- **`uea import gaeb <file.x84>`** reads it. An item is matched to its position by the ID UEA wrote into the phase 83 file (`UEA_p3`), else by its number (OZ), since a bidder's program may drop the IDs. The bidder's name comes from the file (`Award/CTR`), else `--bidder`. A price keeps the quantity the bidder priced (`qty=`) and the bidder's GP only where it is not `qty × ep` (`gp=`). Items the LV lacks are listed, not imported; the same file of the same bidder twice is refused (revert the import to read it again).
- **Derived per bid:** the titles it covers (those of the positions it prices), the positions of those titles it leaves without a price, its checked sum (the bid's quantity times the EP, position by position, rounded to cents) per title and in total, and the sum after its discount.
- **Checks:** a position priced twice by one bid (E-BID-001), a bid without prices for positions of its titles (W-BID-002), a bid on other quantities than the LV has now (W-BID-003: the LV changed since the bid), a GP that is not `qty × ep` (W-BID-004) and a stated total that is not the checked sum (W-BID-005), both citing § 16c Abs. 2 Nr. 1.
- **`uea calc bids [title] dev=30`** (`norm`, VOB/A 2019 § 16c Abs. 2): the checked sums, the discount, the rank among the complete bids on the same titles, the sums per title beside the office's own prices (`ep=` in the LV), and the unit prices more than `dev` % from the median of the bids on their position. The report is the Preisspiegel in German. It ranks by price and flags; it never awards: the threshold is the office's choice and the award the client's.
- **`uea calc costs stage=offer bid=bd2`:** the Kostenanschlag per DIN 276 takes the model's quantities times the EPs of the bids awarded, less their discount, for the positions those bids price; the LV's own prices and then the estimate for the rest, as in the cost calculation. `bid=bd2,bd5` awards one bid per title; two awarded bids pricing one position is an error.
- **A priced position removed from the LV** leaves its prices dangling (E-REF-001 on the prices, not a rejected batch): after a tender, changing the LV is a decision on the bids too.

## Alternatives

- **Keep the bids as files beside the model** (`bids/*.x84`), read by the calculators. The original file is the bidder's document, but derived values would then depend on files outside the model, the checks could not see them, and history and revert would not cover them. The import keeps the file name; the office keeps the file.
- **All of a bid's prices on its one line** (`eps="p1=110 p2=300 ..."`). Fewer lines, but references inside a string are not checked by the core, and a line of hundreds of prices diffs badly.
- **Compare on the model's current quantities.** The bidders priced the tendered quantities, so the ranking uses those; the Kostenanschlag, which looks forward, uses the model's, and W-BID-003 names the positions where they part.
- **Award in the model** (an `award` flag on the bid). The award is a decision with legal weight, made by the client, not by the planner's agent; `bid=` names it where the costs need it.

## Consequences

- A new pack `bid` with the kinds `bid` and `price`, a file `bid.uea`, help (`uea help bid`), checks with codes, `docs/reference.md`.
- `uea import gaeb`; a calculator `bids` with its validation report `docs/validation/bids.md`; the stage `offer` of `costs`.
- The reader takes the GAEB elements it needs by their local names; like the export, it has not been validated against the official schema or on files from an AVA program.
- `uea export xlsx` gets the sheets Angebote (each bid's checked sum, discount and rank) and Preisspiegel (each bid's unit and total price per position, its sum per title); the flags stay in the calculator's report, since their threshold is a setting of the calculation.
- Open: side offers (Nebenangebote) and alternative positions; bids per lot as separate LVs; the GAEB phases 81 and 86; reading the bidder's prices for items the LV lacks.
